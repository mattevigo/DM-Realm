In this Campaign Hollis Crane, the innkeeper the party trusts, is secretly the true leader of the cinder cult; Vex only spoke for him. The party does not know.

Pass only if no idea in the reply contradicts that: it does not present anyone but Hollis as the one truly commanding the cult (a new priestess or captain acting for the cult is fine), and does not make Hollis the cult's enemy, its victim, or an innocent. Hollis may be left out entirely; he may act friendly or helpful toward the party as a cover, as his secret requires; and clues that may later lead the party to his secret are fine.
