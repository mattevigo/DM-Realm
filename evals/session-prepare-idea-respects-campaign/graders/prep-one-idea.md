This is the Session note an agent wrote after offering the DM three ideas for what the cinder cult does to the party at the start of the Session, the DM having asked for the first one in the Prep.

Pass only if all of these hold for the note:

1. Its Prep holds one idea for what the cult does at the start of the Session — not a list of two or three alternatives to choose from.
2. That idea keeps Vex (the cult's high priestess, killed last Session) dead, does not count on Kell (the wizard) casting a spell that needs a spell slot, and does not make Hollis Crane anything but the cult's secret leader, if it names him (clues that may later lead the party to his secret are fine).

Other Prep content — where the party stands, the previous Session's loose threads, links to the Adventure — is fine.
