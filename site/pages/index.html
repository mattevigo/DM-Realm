What it does

A co-DM for the paperwork

You keep running the table. DM Realm keeps the notes that hold your campaign together, and asks before it changes anything.

2014 or 2024 rules

Pick the Edition at Setup. Every rule, spell and monster you bring in follows it, and anything from the other Edition is marked as such.

Any language

Folders, file names and notes are written in the language you choose, using the publisher's official translation of each game term where there is one.

Imported, not invented

Spells, monsters and items come from one machine-readable source, cited by book and page. Nothing official is written from the model's memory.

The whole Session loop

Prepare a Session, take notes at the table, close it into a recap and Character updates, then tell it as a Chapter for your players.

How it behaves

Three promises

Your notes stay yours

Everything is plain Markdown in a folder you own. Live Notes are kept word for word, and Templates you edit are never overwritten.

Preview, then a yes

Setup, Consolidation and every bigger change show you exactly what they will write, and wait for your go-ahead.

Sources, not memory

Rules answers name the book and page, and the House Rules that change them. What the source does not hold is never made up.
