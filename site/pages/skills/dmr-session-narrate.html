> /dm-realm:dmr-session-narrate <the Session, with any instruction>

Ask it like this

  • narrate session 5
  • write the chapter of the last session
  • tell it as a bard would, and keep that style

How

  1. Finds the Session, which must be consolidated.
  2. Reads only its Present line, Live Notes and Recap, the notes they link to, the previous Chapter and the Voice. Never the Prep.
  3. Holds back what the party never learned.
  4. Writes the Chapter in the Chronicle folder: # Chapter 3: The Gatehouse, a link to the Session, then the prose.
  5. Saves an instruction to the Voice when you say "from now on" or "keep that style".
  6. Reports gaps in the Chronicle and each fact held back.

Never

  • Invents an event, an outcome, or a Character's thoughts beyond what the player gave.
  • Uses game mechanics, unless your Voice asks for them.
  • Replaces an existing Chapter without your yes after a warning.

Guide: Tell the story.
