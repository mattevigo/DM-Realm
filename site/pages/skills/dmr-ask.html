Ask it like this

  • what should I do now?
  • how do I bring in my old vault?
  • what happened with Tessa?
  • how hurt is Brom?
  • can Durga grapple the ogre from last session?
  • what could the cult do next?

What it answers

QuestionAnswered from
Using DM Realm, what to do nextThe skills' own descriptions, how they fit together, and your Workspace as it stands. Names the command to run.
What happened, where things standLive Notes and Recaps; Party State and Builds. Names each note.
Rules and official materialThe Trusted Source, in your Edition, with book and page and the House Rules that change it.
Running the gameIts own advice, labelled as advice; mechanics checked against the source.
Ideas for your storyInvented on request, checked against your rules and the Campaign, marked as invented.

Outside a Workspace it answers only questions about using DM Realm, and offers Setup for the rest.

Never

  • Writes, moves or deletes anything, not even a Glossary row. If something is missing or wrong, it names the skill that fixes it.
  • Runs another skill without your yes.
  • Treats a Chronicle Chapter or a Session's Prep as what happened.
