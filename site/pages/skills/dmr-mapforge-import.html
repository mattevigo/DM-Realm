claude
> /dm-realm:dmr-mapforge-import <the Session>

Ask it like this

  • import the MapForge log into session 21
  • bring MapForge's record into the last session

How

  1. Finds MapForge's Session Logs in the Workspace's .mapforge/ folder, with their dates and which Campaign each belongs to.
  2. Matches them to the Session by its date. A Session with no date gets a question.
  3. Writes each log as a ### MapForge, <date> <time> subsection after your own Live Notes: Maps, comments word for word, rolls with DCs, moves grouped into one line between two other events, and each fight: its end state per combatant, then its notes, rolls and moves in the order they were played.
  4. Reports how many comments, rolls, moves and fights each log brought in.

Never

  • Changes anything but the Session's Live Notes, and there only adds.
  • Writes to MapForge's files.
  • Writes a log twice, a log of another Campaign, or a log still running unless you say so.

Guide: At the table.
