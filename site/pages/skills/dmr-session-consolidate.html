claude
> /dm-realm:dmr-session-consolidate <the Session>

Ask it like this

  • consolidate the last session
  • consolidate session 13 of Ashfall
  • close the last session, apply everything without asking

How

  1. Finds the Session: the one you name, or the latest with Live Notes and no Diary entry.
  2. Reads its Live Notes, the only source. Empty ones get a question, and your answer becomes the Live Notes.
  3. Works out every change: who played, Builds, Party treasure, Party State, new NPCs/Places/Quests/Factions, Imports, Promotions, finished Quests, two to four titles.
  4. Shows them all as lines you can drop, and waits.
  5. Applies what you kept, the Diary entry last.
  6. Reports, and offers to narrate the Session.

Never

  • Changes the Session's Prep or Live Notes.
  • Levels up a Character: the report points you to dmr-character.
  • Records anything the Live Notes do not say.

Guide: Close the Session.
