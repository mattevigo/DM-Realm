claude
> /dm-realm:dmr-import <what to import>

Ask it like this

  • add the Goblin
  • import Fireball, Shield and Misty Step
  • import the Wizard and the School of Evocation
  • import all the Conditions

What it imports

Spells, monsters, magic items and equipment, feats, classes and subclasses, class options (invocations, maneuvers…), species, backgrounds, conditions, actions and rules, and Setting lore. One entry per note, one at a time; the only sets it brings in whole are every Condition and every Action.

How

  1. Finds the entry in the Trusted Source, in your Edition's version.
  2. Asks, once, about anything that needs you: an Off-Edition player option, a name that matches several entries, a stat block name already taken.
  3. Renders it with the plugin's helper, never from memory: text, description, images, statistics.
  4. Translates it, recording each term in the Translation Glossary.
  5. Writes the note and its images, then rebuilds the Spell Indexes and names the Portraits of monsters and items.

Good to know

  • Specific magic weapons like "+1 Longsword" import their generic variant ("+1 Weapon"), and Claude says so.
  • A 2024 lineage (Drow) imports its species (Elf).
  • Items say item: true in their frontmatter, with their picture as their Portrait, so MapForge's Treasury lists them.
  • Pasted text is never written in: the entry is looked up by name instead, or offered as Homebrew.
  • Already imported notes are left as they are.

Guide: Bring in official material.
