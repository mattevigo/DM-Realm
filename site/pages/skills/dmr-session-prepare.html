> /dm-realm:dmr-session-prepare <the Session, with your idea>

Ask it like this

  • prepare the next session
  • prep session 12: they reach the crypt, and the cult strikes first
  • a side session for Ayla and Brom called The Mystery of Ambika
  • prepare the next session and give me some ideas

How

  1. Reads the Campaign: README, Sessions, Diary, Party State.
  2. Picks the Session: the next number, the one you name, or a Side Session.
  3. Checks the previous Session is consolidated, and asks if it is not.
  4. Writes the note from the Session Template, with Present and Prep filled in: where the party stands, every loose thread, your idea, links to the Adventure's notes for where the party is.
  5. Reports, offering to import anything the Prep needs that is missing.

Never

  • Writes anything but the new Session note.
  • Puts a fact in the Prep that no source gave.
  • Overwrites a note already at that path, or leaves a gap in the numbering.

Guide: Prepare a Session.
