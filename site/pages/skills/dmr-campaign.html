claude
> /dm-realm:dmr-campaign <the Campaign to start>

Ask it like this

  • start a new campaign called Heroes of the Border
  • a campaign in my world Orsenna, based on The Sunken Keep
  • start Ashfall, and suggest three pitches

How

  1. Takes the name (the only thing it needs) and whatever else your request gives.
  2. Creates Campaigns/<name>/ with NPCs, Sessions, Places, Quests, Factions and Attachments, and the README.
  3. Asks once about every README section you left open, all optional.
  4. Fills in each answer, and offers to create any party member who is not a Character yet.

Never

  • Replaces or merges into an existing Campaign of the same name.
  • Writes Session, Place or NPC notes, or anything outside the Campaign folder.
  • Fills a section on its own: Ideas are offered in chat and only the one you pick is written.

Guide: Start a Campaign.
