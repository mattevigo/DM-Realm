> /dm-realm:dmr-character <what to do>

Ask it like this

  • a new character for Baldu
  • Durga reaches level 6
  • Durga attunes the belt
  • Durga retires
  • transfer Ayla from my old vault

Four jobs

JobWhat it does
CreateA single-class Character at any level, every choice checked against your Edition's rules, Guided (one choice at a time) or Recap (all at once).
Level up / rebuildKeeps the current Build as a past Build, then applies the new level's choices or the change, numbers recomputed.
UpdateEquipment, attunement, prepared spells, coins, backstory, appearance, player, status.
TransferBrings a Character in from an old vault note or another Workspace, after a preview of what comes and what stays behind.

The Character note

Characters/<Name>/<Name>.md: a stat block, then Identity, Build and Statistics. Frontmatter holds only player, status, level and statblock. It records maximums only: what is spent lives in each Campaign's Party State.

Never

  • Writes what happened at a table onto the Character: that goes in the Campaign, linking to it.
  • Names a Campaign or a Session in the Character note.
  • Changes the source of a Transfer.

Guide: Create the party.
