claude
> /dm-realm:dmr-player-wiki <what to do>

Ask it like this

  • make a wiki for my players
  • update the Player Wiki
  • put the World in the wiki too
  • give my players their character sheets
  • "Balance notes" is a DM heading too

How

  1. The first time, proposes what goes in. That is Reference, Homebrew, World, Characters and each Campaign's Chronicle, the headings that mark your secrets, and a folder outside the Workspace. It saves your answer in the Workspace Config.
  2. Runs a script that copies those notes into the folder, rewritten from scratch.
    • A link to a note left out becomes plain text.
    • A monster's stat block becomes readable Markdown.
    • Each Character gets a Character Sheet: a page of its own in the style of a D&D character sheet, made from its stat block, that reads well on a phone. Its note links to it.
    • World and Homebrew notes lose their Secrets and DM Notes sections and their %% comments %%.
  3. With the website chosen at Setup, builds the wiki with Quartz into a folder of HTML pages, and gives you the command to preview it.
  4. Reports what went in, and every section it removed, note by note.

Never

  • Puts in an Adventure, a Session, a Campaign's NPCs, Places or other notes, the Voice, a Past Build, DM Tools or the Templates.
  • Changes a note of your Workspace, or writes the wiki inside it.
  • Empties a folder it did not create.
  • Publishes the site: where it goes is yours to choose, and yours to answer for.
  • Rewrites a word of your notes: the copying is a script, not Claude.

Guide: Share with your players.
