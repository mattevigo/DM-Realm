The Chronicle is your Campaign told as a story for its players, one Chapter per Session. Once a Session is consolidated, dmr-session-narrate writes its Chapter.

  • narrate session 5
  • write the chapter of the last session
  • tell it as a bard would, and keep that style

Faithful, not invented

A Chapter tells only what the party saw or learned, from the Session's Live Notes and Recap (the Live Notes win where the two disagree). Only the telling is invented: the light and sound, the mood, the pace, the wording of a line the notes record. Never an event or an outcome:

  • no fight, find, meeting, wound or death the notes do not give, and none ending differently;
  • nothing a Character decides, thinks or feels beyond what its player gave it at the table;
  • a Character who was not at the Session is not in its Chapter.

Facts the party never learned ("they didn't notice the cultist in the crowd") are held back and listed for you, with an offer to tell them if you want.

The default telling

  • In the Workspace Language, past tense, third person, following the party.
  • No game mechanics: "the blade nearly felled her", not "she took 14 damage".
  • It opens where the previous Chapter ended and ends where the Session did.
  • About 800 to 1,500 words, longer for an eventful Session.

Set the Voice

Tell it how you want the story told. An instruction for one Chapter is used once; say "from now on" or "keep that style" and it is saved in the Campaign's Voice note, used for every Chapter after.

  • narrate session 6, from Ayla's point of view
  • from now on, tell every chapter as a tavern bard would, in the present tense
  • keep chapters under 600 words

The Voice is a note in the Chronicle folder, in your words, one instruction per line. Edit it by hand any time. It can change the person, tense, length, links, even reveal what only the DM knows. It can never make the story invent events.

Rewriting a Chapter replaces it

Ask for a Chapter that already exists and Claude warns you that writing it again replaces the note, including any edits you made by hand, and waits for a yes.
