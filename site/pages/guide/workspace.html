Every note in a Workspace lives in one of eight top-level folders. The folder a note is in is its Scope: what kind of content it is, and what it is allowed to know about. You can add your own subfolders inside them freely; the eight themselves are fixed.

The eight folders

DM Workspace/
├── workspace-config.yml    # language, Edition, folder names
├── Reference/              # official material, imported from the source
├── Adventures/             # one folder per official published adventure
├── Homebrew/               # your own rules, spells, items, monsters
├── World/                  # your own worlds: places, NPCs, pantheons
├── Characters/             # the players' Characters, one folder each
├── Campaigns/              # one folder per Campaign: everything that happens at the table
├── DM_Tools/               # checklists, random tables, prep aids
└── Templates/              # blank notes Obsidian copies from

In a Workspace in another language the folders carry translated names (Riferimento, Campagne…), but they play the same eight roles.

Inside a Campaign

Campaigns/Heroes_of_the_Border/
├── README.md           # pitch, world, Adventures run, party, tone, current state
├── Diary.md            # every Session in play order, a line or two each
├── Party_State.md      # hit points, spent slots and uses, shared treasure: now
├── Sessions/           # one note per Session: Prep, Live notes, Recap, Loose threads
├── Chronicle/          # the story told as prose, a Chapter per Session
├── NPCs/  Places/  Quests/  Factions/
└── Attachments/        # maps, handouts, images

Why the folders matter: links

A note may only link to (or even mention) what its Scope is allowed to know. The rule of thumb:

  • Books know nothing about your table. A spell in Reference never names a Campaign or a Character. "The party first saw Fireball in Session 8" goes in the Session 8 note, linking to Fireball.
  • Characters know nothing about any table either. They can play in any Campaign. An item attuned in Session 21, a wound or an oath is written in that Campaign's notes, linking to the Character.
  • Campaigns can link to everything. That is where your story lives.

So "who has this feat?" or "which Session did this monster appear in?" is answered by Obsidian's backlinks, not by notes written into the feat or the monster.

Ask for a forbidden link and you get a better one

Ask Claude to note on the Goblin's page that your party killed its chief, and it will write that in the Campaign instead, linking to the Goblin, and tell you where it put it.

Promotion

Something you made for one Campaign (a homebrew spell, an NPC) can graduate to Homebrew or World once another Campaign needs it. Ask for it in plain words: Claude confirms, moves the note and removes every mention of the Campaign from it.
