Before you play, ask for the next Session. dmr-session-prepare creates its note in the Campaign's Sessions folder and fills in the Prep, so you start from everything the Campaign already knows.

  • prepare the next session
  • prep session 12: the party reaches the flooded crypt, and the cult strikes first
  • a side session for Ayla and Brom called The Mystery of Ambika
  • prepare the next session of Ashfall, and give me some ideas

The Session note

Every Session note follows the Session Template that Setup wrote (and that you can edit):

---
number: 12
date: 2026-11-14
---
# Session 12

**Present:** [[Ayla]], [[Brom]], [[Durga]]

## Prep
### Strong start · Scenes · Secrets and clues · NPCs · Places · Encounters · Treasure
## Live notes        ← you, at the table
## Recap             ← written at Consolidation
## Loose threads     ← written at Consolidation

Where the Prep comes from

Every fact in the Prep comes from one of these, and nothing else:

  1. Party State: who is wounded, who has spent slots or uses, in a line or two.
  2. The previous Session's loose threads, every one, in that Session's own words, with a link to it.
  3. Your idea, placed in the section it fits: an opening scene under Strong start, an NPC under NPCs.
  4. An Idea you pick, if you ask for ideas: Claude offers two or three in chat, checked against your rules and what the Campaign has made true, and writes only the one you choose.
  5. The Adventure the Campaign is running: links to the notes for the part the party has reached, each with a line on why it matters tonight.

Material the Prep needs that the Workspace does not have yet (a monster an encounter names, say) is listed in the report, with an offer to import it.

Consolidate first

If the last Session you played has not been consolidated, its loose threads are not written yet. Claude warns you and asks whether to consolidate it first or prepare anyway.

Numbers, names and Side Sessions

  • The next number is the highest one in use plus one. Asking for a number that is taken, or that leaves a gap, gets a question rather than a note.
  • A Side Session is played outside the numbered run, often with only part of the party. It has a name instead of a number and takes its place among the others by date.
  • Give a date ("on the 14th") and it goes in the note's properties; otherwise Claude asks for it at the end.

Preparing writes only the new Session note. The Diary, Party State, the README and the Characters stay as they are.
