The Player Wiki is a copy of the parts of your Workspace your players may read, written into a folder outside the Workspace. dmr-player-wiki writes it, and writes it again whenever you ask, typically after a new Chapter.

  • make a wiki for my players
  • update the Player Wiki
  • leave the Characters out of the wiki

What goes in

You choose whole parts. The first time, Claude proposes all of them and asks where the folder should go.

  • Reference, Characters and each Campaign's Chronicle, as they are.
  • Homebrew and World, minus their Secrets and DM Notes sections and their %% comments %%. Keep secrets under another heading? Tell Claude it is a DM heading too.

Never Adventures, Sessions, a Campaign's other notes, the Voice, Past Builds, DM Tools or the Templates. A link to a note left out becomes plain text, so the wiki has no broken links.

A copy, written by a script

The copying is a script, not Claude, so nothing is reworded, and your Workspace is never changed. The folder is rewritten from scratch at every update: a note you delete leaves the wiki too, and an edit made in the wiki is lost. After each update you get a report of what went in and of every section removed, note by note. Look it over before you share.

Keep it private

Reference holds official text. Share the wiki with your players only, never as a public site.

Reading it

The folder is plain Obsidian Markdown. Your players can open it as a vault in Obsidian. You can also build it into a website with a wiki generator such as Quartz and host it where only your players can log in. DM Realm does not publish it for you yet.
