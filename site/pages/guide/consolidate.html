After the Session, close it. dmr-session-consolidate reads the Live Notes, works out everything the Session changed, shows you the full list, and writes only what you keep.

  • consolidate the last session
  • consolidate session 13 of Ashfall
  • close the last session, apply everything without asking

The preview

Every change is a line you can drop. Nothing is written until you answer.

What it writes

In this order, so a run that is interrupted still reads as not consolidated:

  1. Imports of official monsters, items or spells the Live Notes named.
  2. New Campaign notes for NPCs, Places, Factions and Quests met for the first time, holding only what the Live Notes say.
  3. Promotions, when a Character now holds homebrew made for this Campaign.
  4. Character Builds: items, coins and attunement gained or lost.
  5. Quests that ended.
  6. Party State: each Character's hit points, spent slots and uses, conditions, and the party's shared treasure, as they stand now.
  7. The Session note: the Present line, the Recap, the Loose threads and the title. Its Prep and Live Notes are left exactly as they were.
  8. The Diary, last: the Session's entry. A Session is consolidated once the Diary has it.

The Recap and the loose threads

The Recap is written from your point of view and only from the Live Notes: what the party did and how it ended, real outcomes only, about as long as your previous Recaps. Names that have a note are linked. The Loose threads are what the Live Notes leave open, one line each, and they are what the next Session's Prep starts from.

Levels are not gained here

If the party levelled up, the Recap records it, and the report tells you to ask for the level-up with dmr-character, because a level-up has choices that are yours to make.

Party State and the Diary

  • Party State is one note per Campaign showing the party now: no history, just the current hit points, spent resources, conditions and shared treasure. The history is in the Session notes.
  • The Diary lists every Session in play order with a sentence or two and a link. The detail stays in each Recap.
