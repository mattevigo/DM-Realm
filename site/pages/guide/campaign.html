A Campaign is everything that happens at one table: its Sessions, its party, and the NPCs, places and quests as they are in this game. dmr-campaign creates its folder and a README that describes it.

Name it

The name is the only thing a Campaign needs. Give as much or as little else as you like:

  • start a new campaign called Heroes of the Border
  • a campaign in my world Orsenna, based on The Sunken Keep, starting at level 3

Claude creates the folder, its subfolders (Sessions, NPCs, Places, Quests, Factions, Attachments) and a README with whatever you told it. Then it asks, once, about every section you left open, each marked optional. Answer any of them, or none.

The README

SectionHolds
PitchThe premise, in one to three sentences.
WorldAn official Setting, your own World, or both, linked to their folders when they exist.
Adventures RunEach official Adventure this Campaign runs, which part, and whether it is planned, running or finished.
PartyA link to each Character. Empty until one joins.
Starting LevelThe level the Characters start at.
Tone and Table LimitsThemes and tone; the lines (never at this table) and the veils (only off-screen).
TruthsUp to six short statements that make this world its own: "The gods are silent."
Current StateWhere the story stands. At first, the opening scene and the hook.

Every value is in your words. A section you never answer is simply left out.

An example; the exact wording varies

DM
Start a campaign called Ashfall. Give me three ideas for the pitch.
C

Created Campaigns/Ashfall/ with its README. Three pitches to pick from:

  • A volcano wakes under a trading city, and the guild that sealed it is lying about why.
  • …

The other sections are optional: world, Adventures, party, starting level, tone and limits, truths, opening…

Ideas are offered, never written for you

When you ask for ideas (a pitch, truths, an opening) Claude offers two or three in chat. Only the one you pick or edit goes into the README.

The party

Name a party member who is not a Character yet ("Baldu will play a dwarf") and Claude offers to create the Character with dmr-character, at the Campaign's starting level, then links it in the Party section. That is the next lesson.

Your own plot

A plot you write is never an Adventure: Adventures are only official books. Your quests, villains and dungeons live in the Campaign's own Quests, NPCs, Places and Factions folders. The Templates folder has a blank note for each; ask Claude to create one ("a note for the smuggler Vessa") or create it in Obsidian from the Template.
