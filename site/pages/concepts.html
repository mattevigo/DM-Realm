DM Realm uses a small, fixed vocabulary. Knowing it makes the skills' questions and reports easier to read.

The Workspace

Workspace
One DM's folder of notes, opened in Obsidian as a vault. Set up once, in one language and one Edition. Its workspace-config.yml is what makes it a Workspace.
Workspace Language
The language every folder, file name and note is written in. Chosen at Setup, fixed after.
Edition
The D&D rules the Workspace follows: 2014 or 2024. Fixed once the first official rules material is imported.
Setup
The one-time act that turns a folder into a Workspace; also how it is maintained (dmr-setup).
Scope
What kind of content a note is, given only by the top-level folder it lives in. It decides what the note may link to.
Template
A blank starting note for a kind of content, copied when a new note is made. You may edit them.
Translation Glossary
The note recording how each game term is translated, and whether that is the publisher's official translation, so every note says it the same way.
DM Tools
Notes that help you run the game (checklists, random tables) and belong to no single Campaign.

Official material

Trusted Source
The 5etools data and its images, at one release: the only source official material is imported, translated and researched from.
Source Cache
The local copy of the Trusted Source on your machine, shared by all your Workspaces, refreshed only when you ask.
Import
Bringing one official entry into the Workspace as a translated note (dmr-import). The only way official material enters.
Research
Answering a question about official material from the Trusted Source, with book and page, without writing anything.
Off-Edition Material
Official material of the Edition you did not choose. It only fills a gap, is always marked, and a player option needs your consent.
Spell Index
A note listing the imported spells by level: one of every spell, one per class. Rebuilt automatically.
Stat block
A creature's or Character's statistics as a Fantasy Statblocks fence. Monsters and NPCs get one only if the Workspace chose stat blocks; Characters always do.
Portrait
The one picture of a creature, a Character or an item that MapForge cuts a token face from. A creature's stat block names it; an item note names it in its frontmatter.
Item note
A note of an item, official or your own. It says item: true in its frontmatter, and names its Portrait when it has a picture, so MapForge's Treasury lists it and a Treasure on the Map can be that item.

The Scopes

Reference
Official material imported faithfully, with no knowledge of any Campaign.
Adventure
An official published adventure, kept apart from any Campaign that runs it. A plot you write is never an Adventure.
Part
One top-level division of an Adventure's book: a chapter, an appendix, a dungeon level.
Setting
The lore of a game world. An official Setting is Reference; your own lore is World.
Homebrew
Your game material with mechanics, reused across Campaigns.
World
Your invented story elements (places, NPCs, factions, history) with no mechanics, reused across Campaigns.
House Rules
Your changes to the official rules: in Homebrew for every Campaign, or in a Campaign for that one alone.
Character
A player's character, kept apart from every Campaign so it can play in any of them. NPCs are never Characters.
Build
A Character's game statistics at one moment. One current Build; earlier ones are kept as past Builds.
Transfer
Bringing an existing Character in from outside the Workspace, keeping its identity and Build but not its old table's story.
Campaign
One ongoing game with its own party, Sessions and story: what actually happened at the table.
Promotion
Moving a note made for one Campaign into Homebrew or World once something else needs it, with every mention of the Campaign removed.
Migrate
Bringing a whole old vault into a Workspace. No skill does it yet.

Play

Session
One real-world game meeting of one Campaign, with its Prep, Live Notes and Recap.
Side Session
A Session played outside the numbered run, often with part of the party. It has a name instead of a number.
Live Notes
What you write while the Session is played, kept word for word. The only source of the Recap.
Table Record
What MapForge records while you play: comments, rolls, fights. Once brought into the Live Notes, it is Live Notes.
Consolidation
Closing a played Session: Recap and title, Diary entry, Character changes, Party State.
Party State
A Campaign's one note of where the party stands now: hit points, spent resources, conditions, shared treasure. No history.
Diary
A Campaign's list of Sessions in play order, a sentence or two each.
Idea
Story content Claude invents at your request: never a fact of the Campaign until it is played.
Chronicle
A Campaign's story told as prose for the players, one Chapter per consolidated Session.
Chapter
One Session told as prose in the Chronicle. Not an Adventure's chapter, which is a Part.
Voice
A Campaign's narration instructions for its Chronicle, in your words.
